Before you join

Join only for an eligible request that cannot currently be fulfilled. Joining and pre-approval reserve nothing. You may withdraw freely while waiting.

Automatic assignment and approval

The room-owning faculty approver or equipment custodian must pre-approve. We then recheck current eligibility, staff authority, availability and minimum advance notice before reserving the complete request. Your consent replaces a second offer acceptance. Your requested interval, quantities and mandatory requirements never change automatically. Rooms must remain within the faculty you selected for review. Equipment pickup still requires physical handover.

{% if policy.noticeMinutes %}Current minimum advance notice is {{ policy.noticeMinutes }} minutes. A larger minimum already recorded for your entry is not reduced.{% else %}No minimum advance notice has been configured. Automatic assignment is disabled until an administrator explicitly sets it.{% endif %} Late availability is not automatically booked. Entries expire at their requested start if still unassigned.

Queue positions

First-come-first-served ordering uses the recorded join sequence among eligible requests. Your contextual queue groups the same faculty, resource kind, exact interval and mandatory requirements (including quantities). Before review, position is estimated; after pre-approval it counts eligible pre-approved entries. Other compatible queues can compete for the same resources. A position is not a promise of availability. A request that cannot be fulfilled in full does not block a different feasible request.

Notifications and responsibilities

In-app notifications cover joining, review decisions, first entry into the top three eligible positions, reaching position one, assignment, cancellation and expiration. Minor movements do not send repeated notices. Check your inbox and confirmed reservation details; after confirmation, follow the existing room cancellation or equipment pickup/return workflow. Overdue equipment is never assumed returned.

Privacy and conduct

You see only your own queue information. Current responsible staff see authorized applicants and history. No automatic penalties, financial charges, no-show bans or university-wide restrictions are introduced. No last-minute automatic assignment is used to penalize a student. Any future no-show policy requires separate approval.
