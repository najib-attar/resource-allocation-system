Waiting lists / Policy

Waiting-list policy

Understand the conditions before joining a queue.

What joining means

1

Submit a complete eligible unavailable room or equipment request.

2

Accept the currently configured policy version.

3

Responsible faculty/custodian may preapprove, but no reservation is held.

4

If availability appears, assignment rechecks eligibility, consent and notice limits.

Minimum advance notice must be explicitly configured by administrators; it has no invented default. Positions do not guarantee assignment.
